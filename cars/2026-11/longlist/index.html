Questions for this exact car
  • Is it the preferred petrol automatic, and which T3/T4/other drivetrain and trim?
  • Does it have Sensus Navigation? Ask for a photo of Show Map behind the steering wheel.
  • Does Apple CarPlay actually work? On these Sensus XC40s it was an optional extra on early UK cars and takes up only part of the portrait screen. The CarPlay map stays on the centre display, not the driver's instruments. Owners confirm this; a two-year owner review also calls the stock sat-nav a weak point versus Audi.
  • Confirm price, mileage, service invoices, previous ownership, MOT history, any write-off/import history, and an insurance quotation for the registration.
