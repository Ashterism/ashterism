<div class="car-note" id="definitions"><strong>Important:</strong> This is <em>not</em> a Parkers trade/retail valuation and not an automatic-only comparison. Source: AutoUncle's publicly displayed UK listings data, with year-level <strong>average asking price</strong>, <strong>5th–95th percentile asking-price range</strong>, <strong>median mileage</strong> and number of adverts. Manual/diesel, specification and damaged/imported examples may be present. Figures were checked between 16 and 20 November 2026, with each source's own update date shown. Use these to judge broad value by year, then get a registration/trim/mileage-specific check before buying.</div>
